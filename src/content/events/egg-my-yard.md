Surprise your family on Easter morning! The night before Easter, our volunteers secretly hide candy-filled eggs in your yard so your children wake up to their own egg hunt. Every order helps keep our community pool running.

## Egg packages

- 25 eggs: $25
- 50 eggs: $40
- 75 eggs: $55
- 100 eggs: $65

## A few notes

- Eggs are hidden the evening before Easter.
- Please make sure volunteers can get into your yard.
- Limited to homes within our service area.
