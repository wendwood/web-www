## Egg My Yard – A Secret Easter Surprise!

Surprise your family with a magical Easter morning! Our **Egg My Yard** fundraiser is a fun way to wake up to a yard filled with colorful Easter eggs ready to be discovered.

The night before Easter, our team will secretly hide candy-filled eggs in your yard so your children can wake up to their very own Easter egg hunt!

This fundraiser helps support the Wendwood Pool, so every order helps keep our community pool running strong while creating a memorable Easter surprise for your family.

## Choose Your Egg Package

We offer several egg packages to fit your family:

- 25 Eggs – $25
- 50 Eggs – $40
- 75 Eggs – $55
- 100 Eggs – $65

Each egg will be filled with candy and hidden around your yard for your children to find on Easter morning.

## How It Works

1. Select the number of eggs you’d like.
2. Enter the address where the eggs should be hidden.
3. Leave any special notes or instructions.
4. We’ll handle the rest!

Our volunteers will hide the eggs in your yard the night before Easter so everything is ready for a fun surprise when your family wakes up.

## A Few Notes

- Eggs are hidden the evening before Easter.
- Please make sure your yard is accessible for volunteers.
- This event is limited to homes within our service area.
