## Membership cards and sign-in

Membership cards are emailed as e-passes. Printed passes are available on request. Members scan their card or e-pass at the check-in kiosk. Guest passes are managed through the same system.

## Swim team

The Wendwood Wahoos compete in the GPSA White Division and are open to children included in a pool membership. Swim team fees are separate. See [wendwoodwahoos.com](https://www.wendwoodwahoos.com/).

## Pool rules

- Obey the lifeguards at all times.
- Children under 12 must be supervised by someone 14 or older.
- No running.
- No rough play, spitting, or spouting.
- Diving only in designated areas.
- Swim diapers are required for children who aren't potty trained.
- No alcohol.
- No smoking or vaping.
- No glass.
- No food or gum in the pool.
- No pets.
- No open wounds or contagious illness.
- No inflatables when the pool is full.
- No jumping with inflatables.
- No disruptive behavior.

Management may remove anyone for unsafe or inappropriate behavior.
