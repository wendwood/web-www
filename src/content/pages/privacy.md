This website doesn't have accounts or forms, and it doesn't set cookies.

## Analytics

We count page visits with Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that doesn't use cookies or track you across sites.

## Other services

- **Memberships** are handled by Access Granted Systems. Their privacy policy applies when you sign up.
- **The calendar** is embedded from Google Calendar.
- **Fonts** are loaded from Google Fonts.
- **The site is hosted** on Cloudflare, which processes requests to deliver pages.
